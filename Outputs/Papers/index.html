Preprint Papers:

Haynes, J.D., Jones, A.P., Champion, R., Otkhmezuri, B., Bell, G., Cane, D., Crunkhorn, R., Silverdale, M., Lilleker, J., Carson, A. & Warren, P.A. (2026). Self-Movement and Other-Movement Estimation Involve Distinct Multisensory Processing

This preprint paper outlines the differences between the multisensory (visual and vestibular) processing occurring during self-movement and other-movement estimation of healthy control participants. This paper does not include any patient data and uses a subset of our healthy control data (data collection ongoing).
